Show your refusal to submit to Big Brother in the Lone Star State by way of our handsome t-shirts and/or stickers – with all proceeds benefitting the independent, nonprofit journalism of Caldwell-Hays Examiner, the anti-surveillance-state publication dedicated to the good people of southcentral Texas.

Grassroots organizing in our bi-county area – including by a certain rebellious press corps – has spurred both the City of San Marcos and Hays County (first in TX!) to end their contracts with Flock, while the City of Lockhart flat-out refused to sign onto such an invasive, anti-American proposal. Our Managing Editor has led educational trainings on Flock removal with organizers in Cibolo, Laredo, the Rio Grande Valley and co-hosted a similarly themed presentation at the Take Back Tech convening in Atlanta this spring.
